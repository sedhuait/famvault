Privacy Policy & Zero-Knowledge Promise

Your family wealth never touches our servers.

Famvault has no backend, no analytics SDKs, and no cloud database. Every record, ornament photo, and invoice PDF stays encrypted on your device.

  • 🔐
    On-Device Encryption: Vault keys are protected by your PIN and OS keychain (`SecureStore` / Biometrics).
  • 📷
    Camera & Photos: Used solely to attach ornament photos and purchase bills locally.
  • 💾
    User-Controlled Backups: `.famvault` files are encrypted before you save them to your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive.
